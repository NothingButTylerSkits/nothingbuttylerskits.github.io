GET TO KNOW HIM

About Donald

A chill guy with a surprisingly explosive temper. Just be careful what you do around him!

Donald Duck is one of the MCH characters and a longtime friend of Mickey. Most of the time, Donald is a pretty simple, relaxed, and kind guy. He's generally easy to get along with, as long as everything is going well.

However, Donald has one very noticeable weakness: he can crash out when someone annoys him. It might start with a small tantrum, but that doesn't mean it'll stay small for long.

If someone continues doing something that bothers him while he's already upset, Donald can throw the biggest tantrum imaginable. What started as a little annoyance can quickly turn into a full-on meltdown.

And there's something even more unusual about his crashouts: on rare occasions, Donald might actually turn into a giant monster! It doesn't happen often, but it's definitely something worth knowing about.

Donald and Daisy also have their fair share of arguments. During disagreements, Donald tends to crash out much more than Daisy does. Their relationship has its chaotic moments, even though they care about each other.

But don't let the tantrums fool you. When Donald isn't upset, he's honestly just a chill, kind person. He's not always looking for trouble, and he can be perfectly friendly when nobody is pushing his buttons.

Don't make him crash out. Seriously.

A very important lesson when dealing with Donald Duck.

HIS JOURNEY

How He Started

From becoming part of Mickey's friend group to making appearances throughout the MCH universe.

Donald is part of Mickey's longtime friend group, alongside Minnie and Goofy. As their group grew, Donald eventually met Daisy, and the two became a couple.

Their relationship is not without its disagreements. Daisy can usually handle arguments more calmly, while Donald is much more likely to have a crashout. It's just part of what makes their interactions so entertaining.

As a member of the group, Donald is also part of the larger clubhouse life shared by Mickey and their friends. The group has continued spending time together as they've grown older.

Donald has become a recurring character throughout the MCH universe, appearing in all MCH specials and episodes. Whether he's having a normal day or getting caught in another argument, there's always a chance for something chaotic to happen.
