GET TO KNOW HER

About Minnie

A sweet and lovable character with a side that can sometimes catch everyone off guard.

Minnie Mouse is one of the MCH characters and Mickey's girlfriend. She's often seen as a sweet, adorable, and charming person. She has a lovable personality that makes her easy to get along with.

But there's more to Minnie than just being cute. Sometimes, she can go from a complete cutie patootie to acting absolutely unhinged. Her unpredictable personality means you never really know what you're going to get.

Minnie doesn't always act like she's being dramatic or over-the-top, either. Sometimes, her reactions and behavior are completely genuine. There's a difference between when she's putting on a performance and when she's actually being herself.

Despite her occasional chaotic moments, Minnie genuinely cares about the people close to her. She's especially fond of Mickey, whom she's loved since she was much younger.

Minnie also has ambitions of her own. She doesn't simply want to stand beside Mickey while he chases his dreams. She wants to become a star herself, make a name for herself, and achieve something alongside the person she loves.

Whether she's being adorable, spending time with her friends, pursuing her dreams, or surprising everyone with her unpredictable behavior, Minnie definitely knows how to make an impression.

Don't let the cuteness fool you.

Minnie has a few surprises up her sleeve.

HER JOURNEY

How She Started

From childhood crushes to finding love and eventually becoming part of the NothingButTyler Skits universe.

When Minnie was younger, she had quite an interest in boys. Everywhere she looked, there always seemed to be another pretty boy around. But there was one person who stood out above everyone else: Mickey Mouse.

Minnie had feelings for Mickey for a long time, but she never really got around to telling him. As they grew older, those feelings remained, even as she kept them to herself.

Eventually, near the end of high school, Mickey decided to confess that he liked Minnie a lot. It turned out that Minnie felt the same way! She confessed her feelings too, and the two officially decided to become a couple.

Their relationship became an important part of both their lives. They weren't just dating; they also shared a dream of becoming stars. Mickey wasn't the only one who wanted to make it big. Minnie wanted that future just as much.

Around this time, the NothingButTyler Skits Studio was being introduced to people who might be interested in joining. What happened next was quite unexpected.

The entire gang showed up! Mickey, Minnie, Daisy, Goofy, Donald, Clarabelle, members of the TSC gang, and eventually plenty of others joined too.

I was genuinely shocked by how many people became interested. What started as an attempt to introduce the studio to potential members eventually became the beginning of something much bigger.

And that's how Minnie became part of the NothingButTyler Skits universe, alongside Mickey and the rest of the gang.
