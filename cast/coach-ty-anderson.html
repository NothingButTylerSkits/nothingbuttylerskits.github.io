GET TO KNOW HIM

About Coach Ty

More than just a familiar face behind the camera.

Before joining the NothingButTyler Skits universe, Coach Ty Anderson spent approximately seven to eight years coaching gymnastics. He helped teach young students gymnastics moves, skills, and tricks.

Eventually, Ty decided it was time to move on from coaching. After hearing about his decision, I asked him if he would be interested in becoming a co-director and actor alongside me and the rest of the cast.

After thinking it over for a couple of days, Ty came back with his answer: yes! The cast was absolutely thrilled. Everyone cheered, carried him up, and celebrated his arrival. It was one of those moments that made joining the cast feel extra special.

Although acting wasn't necessarily his thing, co-directing turned out to be a perfect fit. His coaching experience brought a recognizable personality to the production process.

Some people might mistake his directing style for being strict or rude, but that's simply part of how he coaches. Outside of that, he's a kind-hearted person who enjoys life and genuinely cares about what he does.

"Cut, cut, cut!"

THE BEGINNING

How He Started

A gymnastics show, a little imagination, and the beginning of an iconic character.

Coach Ty Anderson's story in the Skits universe actually goes back to when my sister and I were little. We used to put together these little "shows", pretending we were actors and creating our own scenes.

One of those shows was based around gymnastics. I imagined lots of different students and characters for it, including a silly, random, nerdy kid. I also acted as some of the girls in the show.

Somewhere along the way, Coach Ty Anderson randomly popped into my head as a character for the gymnastics show. What started as an idea for a little childhood performance eventually became something much bigger.

Today, Coach Ty is an iconic part of not only the NothingButTyler Skits universe, but also a person who has made a memorable impact in real life.
