GET TO KNOW HIM

About Nurse

A helpful face at Crossroads City Hospital.

Nurse might not be known for a whole lot just yet, but he's already had some important moments in the NothingButTyler Skits universe. He's a kind, chill nurse who works at Crossroads City Hospital in Crossroads City, CI.

One of his most memorable moments happens in MCH MRTDTS 1, where he helps save Bill's life after Copsillean Mouse creates a dangerous situation involving a murder tase.

Nurse also returns in MCH MRTDTS 2. This time, he briefly takes on a front desk role at the hospital, before helping out with Goofy and Daisy as all sorts of chaos unfolds.

Even when things get a little unpredictable, Nurse remains a helpful presence around the hospital. He's one of those characters who is simply there to lend a hand when needed.

One thing worth noting: Nurse's actual name hasn't been revealed in any of the skits so far. For now, everyone just knows him as Nurse!

Always here to help :)

ON THE JOB

At Crossroads City Hospital

From helping patients to handling the front desk, Nurse has had a few different responsibilities.

Nurse works at Crossroads City Hospital, located in Crossroads City, CI. He's one of the hospital's familiar faces and is always ready to help when something comes up.

In MCH MRTDTS 1, Nurse plays an important role when Bill needs help. His actions make this one of the most memorable moments involving the character.

In MCH MRTDTS 2, viewers get to see another side of his hospital work when he briefly works at the front desk. He also helps Goofy and Daisy during their hospital visit.
