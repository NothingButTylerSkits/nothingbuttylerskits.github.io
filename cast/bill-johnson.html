About Bill

A character with an unexpected story and an even more unexpected friendship.

Basement Bill Johnson, mostly just known as Bill, is a character Mickey discovers in the first ever MCH Special: Mickey Refuses To Do The Dishes. Before meeting Mickey, Bill had been homeless for around ten years.

Bill and his brother, Upstairs Mike Johnson, had been struggling financially. After robbers stole all their money from their homes, the two brothers tried signing up for jobs. Unfortunately, nobody would accept them because of their appearances. Eventually, both brothers gave up looking for work and started begging for money to survive.

Despite their circumstances, Bill and Mike chose to spend the money they received on food rather than alcohol. They regularly visited a grocery store where the employees were incredibly kind to them. On one occasion, Bill was just one dollar short when purchasing his food, but the employees still let him buy it and even gave him a few extra dollars to help him out. They showed Mike the same kindness.

Unfortunately, Bill and Mike eventually had an argument about something that has never been revealed. The disagreement caused them to split up, and Bill hadn't seen his brother for a very long time.

During his time homeless, Bill lived under a trampoline that had dirt and plants growing beneath it. He had a couple of blankets to keep warm and even used dirt as a pillow. He also carried Club Crackers™ as food to help keep himself from going hungry.

One day, someone unexpectedly discovered where Bill was staying. That someone was Mickey Mouse himself!

Mickey had run away from Minnie after refusing to do the dishes. After explaining what happened, Mickey and Bill began hanging out. Mickey learned so much about Bill's life, including what he did for a living, how he found food, and how he managed to survive. Their unexpected meeting turned into a genuinely wholesome friendship.

Things took a dramatic turn when Bill decided to help Mickey fight Minnie. During the conflict, Copsillean Mouse tased Bill with a murder tase. In the MCH universe, murder tases are ten times worse than ordinary tasers used by police, and Bill was left in serious danger.

Mickey quickly called a hospital, and Bill was taken to the emergency room. After the battle, Mickey returned to check on him. Eventually, Bill recovered and was able to hang out with Mickey again.

Mickey and Nurse both played important roles in saving Bill's life. Without Mickey finding him, Bill might have continued living under that trampoline for much longer.

Thankfully, Bill's life eventually changed. He now has a home and continues living with Mickey. Their friendship didn't end with the first special either — Bill is still with Mickey in MCH MRTDTS 2!

Sometimes, an unexpected friendship can change everything.

A fitting description of Bill's journey from the first MCH special onward.

HIS JOURNEY

How He Started

From a difficult life under a trampoline to becoming one of Mickey's friends.

Bill's story begins in the first ever MCH Special, Mickey Refuses To Do The Dishes. At the time, he had been homeless for approximately ten years and was living under a trampoline.

His circumstances were difficult, especially after he and his brother Mike lost their money and struggled to find jobs. Eventually, their paths separated after an argument.

Bill's life took a different direction when Mickey unexpectedly found him. The two began spending time together, and Mickey learned about the challenges Bill had experienced.

After Bill was hospitalized during the conflict with Minnie, Mickey returned to check on him. With help from Mickey and Nurse, Bill recovered and eventually found a home with Mickey.

His story continues in MCH MRTDTS 2, where he is still living with Mickey.
